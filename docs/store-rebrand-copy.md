# Store copy for the rebrand

These are drafts for the existing product records. Apply them with the first verified renamed build; do not create replacement apps or discard unrelated Microsoft draft changes.

## Apple

**Name:** Speaker Volume Bridge  
**Subtitle:** Sync Mac and speaker volume

Speaker Volume Bridge keeps your Mac's audio-output volume and a compatible Sonos speaker in sync over your local network. Use system volume controls, keyboard volume keys or supported audio-device controls. Enable two-way synchronization, synchronize mute, choose a maximum speaker volume and schedule Night Mode on supported speakers.

The app controls volume and supported speaker settings. It does not play, stream, capture or redirect audio. No app account is required. Preferences stay on your computer. Speaker Volume Bridge is independently developed and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Sonos, Inc. Sonos is a trademark of Sonos, Inc.

## Microsoft

**Product display name:** Speaker Volume Bridge

Formerly Sonos Volume Bridge. Keep your Windows audio-output volume and a compatible Sonos speaker in sync over your local network, with optional two-way synchronization and mute control. Choose a maximum speaker volume, configure supported speaker settings and schedule Night Mode on compatible speakers.

This update retains the existing app identity and settings. If the old app is still running, Settings explains how to quit it while synchronization is paused. Conflict notifications are local desktop notifications and depend on your Windows notification settings.

The app does not play, stream, capture or redirect audio. No app account is required. Speaker Volume Bridge is independently developed and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Sonos, Inc. Sonos is a trademark of Sonos, Inc.
