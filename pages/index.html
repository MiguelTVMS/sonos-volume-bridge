LESS FIDDLING. MORE LISTENING.

A familiar way to
turn it up. Or down.

01 / CONTROL

Connect your device volume.

Changes to your selected computer audio output’s volume are reflected on your Sonos speaker. Use system volume controls, keyboard keys, or supported device controls.

02 / SYNCHRONIZE

Keep both sides in step.

Enable two-way synchronization to reflect speaker volume changes on your computer too. You can also choose to synchronize mute.

03 / PERSONALIZE

Find your comfort zone.

Set a maximum speaker volume and choose how volume changes feel, including a gentler curve for finer control at low volumes.

04 / KEEP GOING

Set it. Get on with your day.

Follow your current audio output or select a fixed one. Start at login and reconnect automatically after interruptions.

A FEW CLICKS, THEN BACK TO YOUR MUSIC.

Make the connection.

You’ll need a Sonos speaker on the same local network and a computer audio output with software volume control.

Full installation guide ↗

  1. Install and open.

    Download the package for your operating system from the project’s releases.

  2. Choose your speaker.

    Select the Sonos speaker and the computer audio output you want to connect.

  3. Make it yours.

    Choose your sync and volume preferences. Close the settings window and use your normal volume controls.

⌂ON YOUR NETWORK

GOOD TO KNOW

Volume control.
That’s the idea.

This app synchronizes volume and optional mute state. It does not play, stream, capture, or redirect audio.

It’s an early community project, with hands-on testing currently limited to a Sonos Ray. Other models and hardware combinations may need validation.

macOS requires version 13 or later. Linux support targets Ubuntu with PulseAudio or PipeWire with pactl. Check the installation guide for available packages and platform notes.
